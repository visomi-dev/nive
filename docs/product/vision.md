# Nive product vision

Nive is short for Nirvana Economics. Its goal is to help you see which payments
are covered, how much money is left, and what you can start the next budget
period with. You choose how long each period lasts.

## Carry the result forward

Margen represents the balance that carries from one period into the next. What
happened in the current period informs the next plan.

1. **Plan.** Start with the balance available, account for expected income, and
   set aside money for upcoming payments and spending.
2. **Close.** Record what came in and went out, check outstanding payments, and
   confirm the balance available at the end of the period.
3. **Review.** Compare what happened with the plan to see what needs to change.
4. **Use the result.** Bring the available balance and what you learned into the
   next period's plan.

For example, if you end a period with **$800 MXN available**, that balance is
taken into account when you plan the next period. The $800 carries forward only
as an opening balance.
Any income received in the next period is accounted for separately.

## Help with manual entry

The planned agent reduces manual entry. With your permission, it organizes
the bank statements and CFDI invoices you provide and proposes a plan you can
review and adjust before using it.

## What works today

The public website, account setup, first-use welcome and example dashboard are
available. Creating and closing budgets, carrying balances forward, importing
statements and invoices, and getting plan proposals from the agent are part of
the product vision.
